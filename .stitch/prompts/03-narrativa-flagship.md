# 03 — Narrativa flagship

A desktop-first recruiter portfolio where one complex production system establishes depth immediately, followed by three projects that prove range. Content is in Spanish and every claim must be attributable.

**PLATFORM:** Web, desktop-first, fully responsive.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**

1. **Header:** Bastián Ibáñez at left; Proyectos, Experiencia and CV centered or right; GitHub, LinkedIn and ES/EN kept compact.
2. **Hero:** Left-aligned role “Software Engineer · Full-Stack & Applied AI”, a short factual proposition, Santiago and the visible email as sole primary action. Add a direct textual cue to the flagship without a scroll animation.
3. **Flagship case preview:** A large, narrative section titled “Agente de operaciones ERP con IA” and labelled “Trabajo profesional · Caso anonimizado”. Explain the operational problem, Bastián’s end-to-end ownership and why reliable external effects were difficult. Show three concrete decision blocks: controlled ERP writes with human review; durable workflows with idempotency/outbox/recovery; migration from standalone service to reproducible cloud operation. Finish with verified result language: deployed and operated in production, centralizing operational queries and actions. Include a restrained, abstract architecture diagram based only on these facts.
4. **Range through three projects:** Present the manufacturing platform, GroupFit and go-agent in three compositions with unequal hierarchy—not three identical cards. Each includes problem, contribution, evidence and stack.
5. **Professional trajectory:** A concise timeline connecting software engineering studies, NotoriosTI responsibilities, client delivery and open-source/product work.
6. **CV:** A readable CV extract with web and PDF actions.
7. **Contact:** Direct email, GitHub and LinkedIn, no form.

The page should read as a technical story rather than a product landing page. Keep evidence visible, avoid marketing cadence, and never expose private names or fabricate metrics.
