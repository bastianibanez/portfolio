# 04 — Archivo profesional

A desktop-first portfolio homepage designed as a sober professional archive: an explorable record of systems shipped, client work, open-source libraries and experience. It must remain immediately understandable to recruiters.

**PLATFORM:** Web, desktop-first, fully responsive.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**

1. **Archive header:** Bastián Ibáñez and “Software Engineer · Full-Stack & Applied AI” share the first row with Santiago, email and ES/EN. A second row contains Projects, Experience, CV, GitHub and LinkedIn.
2. **Compact introduction:** One factual paragraph and a visible email action. Include a short archive legend explaining truthful statuses: Production, Client delivery, Public product and Open source.
3. **Project archive:** A structured list or table-like composition with four featured entries and secondary records. Columns adapt responsively and include Project, Context, Contribution, Evidence, Stack and Status. Featured entries receive more vertical space and a short synopsis; secondary entries stay compact. Use the approved project hierarchy and never make the list look like an admin data table.
4. **Selected case focus:** Below the archive, expand the flagship “Agente de operaciones ERP con IA” into a concise case preview with Context, Decisions and Result. Use a real abstract system flow, not a screenshot or fake terminal.
5. **Experience ledger:** Chronological records for NotoriosTI, client delivery and studies with exact role/contribution language.
6. **CV record:** Summarize education, languages and core capabilities, with “Ver CV completo” and “Descargar CV PDF”.
7. **Contact footer:** Direct email, Santiago, GitHub and LinkedIn. No form.

The archive should feel curated rather than dense for its own sake. Preserve generous reading space, make every label functional and avoid pills, filtering controls or metrics unless they communicate real information.
